# Privacy policy draft — not ready to publish

Publisher identity, contact details, public URLs, hosting provider and applicable
regional terms must be completed before publication.

Dooms Day App stores reading preferences, bookmarks, preparation checklists and saved
coordinates on your device. These are not sent to an application account server.
There is no account system, advertising SDK or analytics SDK in this version.

The application requests location only when you choose to locate yourself on a
map. Location stays on the device. Sensor readings support the on-screen compass.
You can read the handbook without granting these permissions.

When you request downloads, the hosting provider receives ordinary connection
metadata, including your IP address. Add the actual provider, retention and
processing details before publishing this policy. Opening an external source link
uses your browser and the destination site's policies.

Wikipedia search sends your selected language and search query to Wikimedia only when you explicitly choose online search. Saving or checking updates requests the selected page from Wikimedia. Saved article text, revision and attribution stay in the local database. No automatic online searches or update polling occur. The native Kiwix prototype is not linked into normal app builds. Audit the final archive hosting and transfer flow before enabling that feature.

You can remove saved Wikipedia copies, saved places and bookmarks in the app. Uninstalling removes local
application data subject to platform behavior. Pack and application data are
excluded from backup by the configured platform controls, which require device
verification before release.

Development diagnostics exclude precise location, messages and secrets. Test
records are stored separately in development builds.
